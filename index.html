FAQ

Cooking gas delivery questions

How do I order cooking gas in Port Harcourt?

Send a WhatsApp message to 0810 230 8647 or call any of our numbers. Tell us your area and cylinder size, and we deliver the refilled cylinder to your door.

Which areas do you deliver cooking gas to?

We deliver to Igwurita, Eneka and Elimgbu in Port Harcourt. If you live elsewhere in Port Harcourt, message us on WhatsApp to check whether we can reach you.

Do you refill LPG gas cylinders?

Yes. Son and Father Gas offers LPG refill and cylinder delivery across Rivers State.

Which numbers can I call to order gas?

0810 230 8647 (WhatsApp and calls), 0816 363 9960 and 0707 714 7789.

Is Son and Father Gas a registered company?

Yes. Son and Father Oil and Gas Ltd. is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), RC 743972.
